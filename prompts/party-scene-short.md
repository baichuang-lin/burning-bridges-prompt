# The party-scene prompt, condensed

A shorter version of [`party-scene.md`](party-scene.md), written for lists and
READMEs that cap entry length. The timings are the same; the wording is tighter.
The full text is the one the site actually sends.

```text
An upscale private party room at night: dark wood-panelled walls, one large crystal
chandelier, a bar-height table with green LED under-lighting, teal accent lighting, a
crowd of about twenty dancing behind. Photorealistic live-action, 30fps, one single
continuous handheld take, no cuts, camera at chest height drifting and orbiting
slightly, shallow depth of field on the character. The character stands centre frame
chest-up holding a cup.

IDENTITY LOCK: the face, hairstyle, hair length, colour and hairline must match the
reference photo exactly and stay identical in every frame. Keep whatever clothing is
visible in the photo; if none is visible, dress the subject in plain casual party
clothes and keep those consistent. The subject is fully and modestly dressed in every
frame — this overrides everything else.

ACTION SEQUENCE, timed to the supplied 129.3 BPM track (one beat every 0.464s). Fixed
order; do not invent, skip, reorder or free-style:
0.00-0.97s lean and sway with the beat, cup out to the side.
0.97-1.90s turn to camera, cup in to chest, nodding.
1.90-2.37s lean toward the lens, wave the free hand close to it.
2.37-3.29s stand up straight, singing along, cup in front of the chest.
3.29-4.69s cup up to shoulder height, free arm sweeping across the chest.
4.69-6.54s dance in place, cup up, look around at the crowd, laughing.
6.54-8.86s the big arm swing: free arm out to the side, down and across, twice.
8.86-10.25s bend the arm, free hand up near the face, eyes closing, swaying.
10.25-11.18s raise the cup high with both hands, head tilted back.
11.18-14.43s turn to the woman on the right with the space buns; the two cups clink
twice, then both arms open celebrating.
14.43-17.68s back to the camera, point at it, drink (cup covering the lower face),
lower the cup, smile.
17.68-20.09s wave, then a full cheer — both arms open and up, the whole crowd behind
raising their arms.

AUDIO: the supplied track is the only audio. Do not generate music, ambience, crowd
noise, cheering, clapping, laughter, speech or singing.

STYLE: natural skin texture and body physics, believable fabric, practical party
lighting, 35mm film grain, slight handheld imperfection, realistic motion blur. No
CGI look, no illustration. No on-screen text, captions, subtitles, watermarks or
logos. One continuous shot, 20 seconds, 9:16 or 16:9.
```
